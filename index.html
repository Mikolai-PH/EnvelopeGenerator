Diode calibration

The amplifier and the modulator do not respond proportionally: send an ideal envelope and the diode sees a distorted one. Here you measure that distortion, invert it, and apply the inverse in advance, so the two cancel.

  1. Download the triangle file below and load it into your generator as the output waveform. It is 16384 points, values 0 to 1, one per line.
  2. Run the setup as during a normal measurement: generator, modulator, laser, photodiode.
  3. Capture the photodiode on the oscilloscope over at least one full triangle period and export it as CSV with two columns, time and voltage.
  4. Upload that capture below. The app finds the peak, then the two minima either side, and uses the rising edge between them — that is the only monotonic part, and only a monotonic function can be inverted.
  5. Look at the transfer chart. The blue measured curve should bend away from the dashed y = x line. If it lies on it, your hardware is already linear and no compensation is needed.
  6. Check the quality readout. R² below 0.99 usually means a noisy or clipped capture — repeat it with a cleaner signal rather than trusting the fit.
  7. Leave the compensate box ticked in the export bar. The CSV is then pre-distorted so that the diode sees the envelope you designed.

A triangle is used because any departure from a straight line is visible at a glance, and because its rising edge covers the whole amplitude range exactly once.
